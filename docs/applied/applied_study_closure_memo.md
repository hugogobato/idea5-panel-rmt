# Applied Study Closure Memo

**Date:** 2026-08-29. **Decision served:** finalize Phase D/E applied triage so paper writing can begin without continuing an outcome-driven convenience search.

## 1. Executive decision

The search has now produced all three empirically relevant frontier phenotypes, but no application satisfies the preregistered Gate G4 composite. The applied contribution is therefore **`INCREMENTAL-ONLY` as a scientific finding** and useful **as a diagnostic casebook**. The paper should proceed, if desired, as a simulation-and-theory-led methods paper. It should not promise a flagship applied discovery or claim that a real treated unit was stably located below the BBP strength frontier.

The closest near-hit is Nebraska RTC monthly violent crime: it is the first preregistered treated `FRAGILE-MISALIGNED` case, but seven of eight placebos share that label, P2 and stability fail, the count scale flips the label to `RECOVERABLE`, and donor-post transport rejects. This is a limitation result, not a proper application pass.

## 2. Evidence synthesis

| Empirical role | Panel | Treated label | What survived | Why it is not a flagship |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deep-supercritical positive controls | California smoking, Germany, Basque | `RECOVERABLE` | Correct certification; smoking fit ordering; transport alarms | Absolute `d` saturates; no NF/P2/stability composite |
| Channel-2 asymmetry | Bonander Florida SYG | `RECOVERABLE` | Treated aligned while all 15 placebos misaligned; N3 | P1, P2, and stability fail |
| Disclosed public proxy | CDC bi63 Wisconsin suicide | `RECOVERABLE` | Reproducible small-donor certification | Wrong outcome and time resolution; all placebos recoverable; stability fails |
| Treated misalignment | FBI Nebraska RTC violent-crime rate | `FRAGILE-MISALIGNED` | First preregistered N1; P3 passes | Seven of eight placebos also misaligned; P2/stability fail; scale and transport failures |

Together these panels support a narrower empirical statement: real policy panels often sit far above the strength frontier, so practical fragility operates through treated-factor misalignment, selector scope, and post-window transport rather than through an observed `d<1` strength failure. That statement is supported by the casebook, but it is not the originally preregistered new applied finding.

## 3. Paths that should be closed

The ClawRxiv “audit of 100 studies” is not admissible evidence for candidate selection. The inspected page contains generic placeholder references and no verified Table S1, study list, or replication archive. It cannot carry an E3/E4 decision or justify screening 43 allegedly fragile studies.

The yearly Sweden CO2, Wisconsin suicide, São Paulo homicide, and Moser-Voena patent proxies are already known to be saturated, outcome-mismatched, or outside the geographic SCM observation scheme. Further variants of those panels have low decision value. Unregistered sweeps across many FBI policy dates would create a multiple-search problem and should not be used to manufacture a successful treated label.

## 4. Honest paper path

The applied section should be written as a preregistered diagnostic casebook with three contrasts: 1. deep-supercritical aligned canonical panels, 2. Bonander's treated-placebo alignment asymmetry, and 3. Nebraska's panel-wide, scale-dependent misalignment and transport failure. The headline empirical claim is about where the diagnostic does and does not add information, not about a new policy effect.

The paper's load-bearing claims remain C1 and C3 under the restricted Gate G3 scope. C5 is demoted from headline contribution to scope and failure-mode evidence. A skeptical-referee paragraph should state plainly that no real panel in the search delivered a stable, treated-specific sub-frontier event.

## 5. Only condition for reopening the application gate

Reopen Gate G4 only if an independently motivated, policy-matched micro panel becomes available before its spectral outcomes are inspected. The candidate must have a deposited or legally accessible outcome matching the intervention, a treatment date and donor pool justified by the substantive literature, a leakage-safe coverage audit, and enough placebos for separation. The restricted monthly handgun-suicide data used by Powell would qualify on design if access is obtained. A reconstructed school-district panel could qualify only after a trusted published result is reproducible. Neither is an immediate prerequisite for starting the current methods paper.
