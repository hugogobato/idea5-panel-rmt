**Top-3 fit-for-pipeline notes.**  
São Paulo (rank 1) is closest to ideal: public rectangular state-year homicide panel, classic single treated unit, moderate n_d/T0, crime outcome free of dominant world-cycle factor; minor reshaping (unit centering, ensure T0≥12). Florida SYG / related (rank 3) is ready via OSF CSV; confirm exact donor count and pre-window length, center levels, and note any short-T0 sensitivity. High Point place-based (rank 2) is promising for noisy micro outcomes but may require reconstruction or author files to obtain a clean rectangular donor panel; treatment timing is clear (DMI rollout years).

**3 negative controls (expected clearly supercritical / good-fit contrast).**  
1. California Prop 99 smoking (Abadie et al. 2010) — already saturated, d enormous.  
2. German reunification (Abadie/Hainmueller Dataverse doi:10.7910/DVN/24714) — same.  
3. A well-documented good-fit state-level policy with long pre and large donor pool (e.g., selected “clean” minimum-wage or clean-air applications that papers themselves describe as having excellent pre-MSPE and stable weights; or Basque if re-run only as contrast). These serve as positive controls where the pipeline should flag RECOVERABLE.

**Staggered-adoption candidates (secondary).**  
RTC / shall-issue (Donohue et al.), medical/recreational marijuana legalization panels (Hollingsworth et al. Zenodo/GitHub and related), and selected CAP/SYG staggered analyses. These can be sliced into single-treated-unit windows that meet n_d≥6 / T0≥12; prefer those with documented donor sensitivity or volatile outcomes. Full staggered SDID packages exist but are lower priority unless a paper explicitly flags poor pre-fit for particular cohorts.

**Search notes / caveats.**  
Queries focused on “synthetic control” + (poor/inadequate/high MSPE/RMSPE/sensitivity/donor/Ferman) + replication/Dataverse/GitHub, plus crime/health/micro applications and known tool-paper citations. Many published SCMs claim “good fit” (negative control) or lack deposited rectangular panels. Crime/firearm and place-based applications most frequently document volatility or imperfect matches. Always download and inspect the actual panel (csv/dta) for full pre-window donors, missingness, and dimensions before pipeline use; licenses are typically CC0/academic or public-domain government data. No pure simulations or proprietary microdata included.
