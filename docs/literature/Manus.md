# Search results: fragile synthetic-control and SDID applications

## Bottom line

The search found **four well-supported applications** and four additional screening leads. The strongest first-run candidates are **Kumar and Liang’s Texas credit-constraints panel**, **Gobillon and Magnac’s French enterprise-zone panel**, and **Ho, Ashwood, and Handan-Nader’s Los Angeles restaurant-grading panel**. They use labor, unemployment, or health outcomes rather than national GDP levels, and at least one has a short pre-window or documented low-power/fit concern. The Alaska Permanent Fund study is best treated as a benchmark or negative control unless its replication code reveals unstable pre-treatment fit.

A fully compliant set of 8–15 candidates cannot be claimed without inventing unverified donor counts or archive contents. The table therefore marks candidates as **verified**, **partially verified**, or **screening lead**. This preserves the distinction between facts read from the paper/archive and hypotheses that still require a code/data pass.

## Top-three pipeline assessment

**Texas** is the best initial run. It has a single treated state, a labor-market outcome, and a potentially short 1992–1996 pre-window before the 1997 episode. The two constitutional amendments should be analyzed separately, and donors exposed to comparable credit-law changes should be excluded. The principal risk is a remaining national labor-cycle factor in annual state data.

**French enterprise zones** are the most conceptually valuable case because the paper directly studies the relationship between SCM and interactive factor models for local unemployment. Before running the frozen pipeline, confirm whether treatment is assigned to one local area or several, whether all donor areas are observed throughout the pre-window, and whether the archive supplies a rectangular outcome matrix.

**Los Angeles restaurant grading** is a weak-signal case. Its appendix reports reasonable pre-treatment balance but also documents low power against moderate effects. Annual county rates should be the primary input; monthly hospitalization data should be treated as a separate robustness exercise because sparse outcomes and zeros can distort the spectral noise estimate.

## Staggered-adoption leads

The best staggered-adoption search direction is the **SDID/CPS state-policy family**, followed by Powell’s multi-state minimum-wage work. Health-policy panels with staggered state legislation are a secondary direction, but they require stringent screening for national trends, spillovers, and ragged missingness. At present these are leads, not verified final candidates.

> **Methodological relevance.** Ferman and Pinto show that under imperfect pre-treatment fit, fixed-control SC weights need not recover the treated unit’s factor loadings even as the number of pre-treatment periods grows.[11] This is why the Texas, French regional, and Los Angeles weak-signal cases are more useful for the stated frontier experiment than another canonical macro application.
